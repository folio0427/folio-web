- We use this data to **provide the matching service**: your **mode** drives the post filtering matrix (whether you and others can see each other's posts in Explore and search); your **desired gender** is only a preference signal that the app shows to others according to the filtering matrix, and is **not itself used to filter** whom you see. We do **not** sell it, use it for advertising, or build advertising / marketing profiles from it. Our third-party analytics provider (Firebase) does **not** receive your mode, gender, or desired gender, only age and de-identified usage-flow events, so the sexual-orientation inference **never enters third-party analytics and is never used for advertising**. We do, however, review this data in our own admin console in **aggregate** form (for example the distribution of modes, mode × gender cross-tabs of how matches are composed, and conversion rates at each step of a feature), in order to understand how the service is used and improve the matching mechanism; those statistics are visible only to our administrators, are never sent to any third party, are not used for advertising or personalisation, and are never used to treat an individual user differently.
- You choose to provide this data: at registration you pick one of three modes, and if you pick "Looking for fate" or "Open to either" you enter a desired gender; the current registration flow also asks for your separate, explicit consent to our processing of this data. You may change your mode (7-day cooldown) and desired gender (no cooldown) at any time on the "Me" page.

## 05　Cross-Border Transfer

- The Supabase host is in Singapore; FCM, APNs, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Cloudflare are global; the RevenueCat and Resend hosts are in the US; book cover images are loaded by your device directly from Google Books (global), Open Library (United States), and the National Central Library (Taiwan) (see §04)

- Personal data of Hong Kong users is likewise stored and processed outside Hong Kong (in Singapore and at global edge nodes). Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance currently imposes no data-localization requirement, and its cross-border transfer restriction (Section 33) is not yet in force; we nonetheless handle such transfers in line with the safeguards recommended by the Privacy Commissioner

Under Australian Privacy Principle 8 we remain accountable for the handling by overseas recipients (Supabase in Singapore; RevenueCat and Resend in the United States; Google / Apple at global edge nodes)
- Transit encryption: TLS 1.2+
- At-rest encryption: AES-256
- By using the service, you consent to cross-border transfer

## 06　Retention

### During account lifetime

- Data is retained to provide the service
- Posts and the applications they received: deleted automatically 3 days after the post's deadline (or when you remove the post earlier); co-reading rooms already created keep a snapshot of the post and application text
- Accounts with **no sign-in for 24 consecutive months and no use of the app during that period** (except supporters with an active subscription) are deleted automatically in a monthly routine job, together with their personal data; the scope of that deletion is the same as when you delete the account yourself (see "Account deletion" below). This corresponds to "Platform-initiated termination" in the Terms of Service

### After account deletion

- Personal data is deleted immediately once you confirm "Delete account"; items retained anonymously or under the rules below are described in the sections that follow
- Statutory retention (for example tax) per applicable law; if we receive a preservation request from a court or law-enforcement authority, we will preserve the relevant data to the extent technically feasible

### Specific retention periods

- Violation, report, and related enforcement records (incl. reported content): retained for community safety, abuse prevention, and legal defense. After a report is **closed (actioned or dismissed) it is retained for 5 years and then automatically purged**; reports not yet resolved are kept until handled. The link to your account is removed after you delete your account, but nicknames and content snapshots in those records are kept for the period above
- Moderation action audit records (action type and time, a snapshot of the content at the time of the action, and the identifier of the person acted upon): retained to prevent suspension evasion, for community-safety auditing, and for legal defence; these records are **not removed when an account is deleted**. Once you delete your account, the identifier stored in such a record no longer resolves to any profile on our side
- Suspension-evasion hash (when an account is permanently suspended, a one-way salted SHA-256 hash of its OAuth sign-in identifier, stored together with the original account identifier and the reason and time of the suspension, used to prevent re-registration evasion and to explain the reason to you): auto-deleted 5 years after suspension; removed immediately upon unbanning. See "Suspension Notice & Appeal" in the Terms of Service for details
- Sign-in session records (including IP address and device / browser identifier string): kept until the session ends (for example when you sign out) or your account is deleted
- Raw usage-analytics event records (app open times and counts, feature-tap events, time spent and entry counts per feature screen): retained for the lifetime of your account for product improvement and usage analysis (feature-tap events are kept for 365 days); they are deleted together with your account when you delete it (including the automatic deletion after 24 months without sign-in described above). To evaluate new features, we kept a per-user snapshot of usage status before launching "Everyone's sticky notes", and may keep another when we launch new features in future (activity level, usage counts per feature, sign-up time, mode, gender, community partition, and subscription tier); it is used only for aggregate analysis in our own admin console, never sent to third parties, and deleted together with your account
- Daily active-user list (containing only the date and an account identifier, used to calculate activity and retention statistics): kept after account deletion so that deleting an account does not rewrite historical statistics, with no fixed deletion period; once you delete your account, the identifier it holds no longer resolves to any profile on our side
- Anonymous aggregate statistics (daily counts of sign-ups, active users, posts, messages, co-reading rooms created and unlocked, and similar pure counts, containing **no user identifier**): **retained indefinitely**. Such data can no longer be traced back to any individual and is not personal data
- Payment and subscription event records (plan tier, amount and currency, store country or region code, purchase / renewal / expiry events, store and subscription-service transaction identifiers): kept as financial records for reconciliation, defence against refund disputes, and the retention duties imposed by the Commercial Accounting Act and the Tax Collection Act, with **no fixed deletion period**. Once you delete your account, the link between these records and your profile is removed (user_id set to NULL); the records still hold the store-side transaction identifiers and the account identifier used by the subscription service (RevenueCat); the latter is deliberately kept because it is necessary for investigating refund disputes, and it no longer resolves to any profile on our side
- Data preserved at the request of a court or law-enforcement authority: until those proceedings end
- Terms consent records (`user_tos_consents`): **5 years** (legal evidence; auto-purged on expiry). Records include the `doc_type`, version, timestamp, method of your acceptance, the **IP address and the device / browser identifier string (user-agent) at the moment of your consent**, plus a **salted SHA-256 hash of your email** (salt kept server-side, never held by the client). The hash is **one-way** and cannot be reversed to recover your email. Purpose: (a) after you delete your account, allow us to verify in litigation whether an email you claim is yours ever consented on Folio; (b) without retaining the plaintext email. This constitutes pseudonymisation under GDPR Art. 4(5) and is lawful.
- Terms-change notices: we do not currently keep a separate record of whether terms-change notices were delivered or opened; the terms-consent records above serve as evidence of notice and consent.
- System backups: the database is backed up automatically every day and backups are kept for 7 days; after you delete your account, your data disappears from backups as they rotate
- Timing records used for rate limiting (for example when you liked a room or looked up a friend code; containing no content): may be kept after you delete your account
- Drift bottles: copies of your letters saved by other users remain in the saver's letter box until they delete them; replies and conversations you decline, and content taken down due to expiry or moderation, are moved to an archive (for abuse prevention, operational statistics, and legal defense) and are no longer shown to regular users. **Letter contents in that archive are cleared automatically 2 years after archiving**; only the statistical fields recording type and time (which contain no letter content) are kept long-term for operational statistics
